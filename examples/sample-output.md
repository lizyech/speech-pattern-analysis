### Strongest moments

**1. Framework on the fly**
> "OK so there are three buckets here. Things that are urgent and we know how to solve. Things that are urgent and we don't. And things that can wait but will compound if we ignore them."

- Setting: Strategy discussion, responding to ambiguous ask from senior stakeholder
- Dimensions: Discourse Coherence (5), Directive Precision (4)
- Why it works: Spontaneous framework creation under pressure. Takes a vague problem and gives it structure in real time. The group immediately adopted the framework for the rest of the discussion.

**2. Direct redirect with warmth**
> "I hear you, and that's a valid concern. But let me push back — if we optimize for that, we lose the thing that actually matters to the customer."

- Setting: Strategy discussion, disagreeing with a peer
- Dimensions: Pragmatic Modulation (5), Strategic Fluency (4)
- Why it works: Validates before redirecting. Lands the point in one sentence. No hedging, no dissolution.

**3. Coaching with concrete imagery**
> "Don't think of it as a handoff document. Think of it as a movie trailer — 90 seconds, the best moments, and a reason to watch the whole thing."

- Setting: 1:1 with direct report
- Dimensions: Directive Precision (5), Rescue Deployment (4)
- Why it works: Concrete metaphor that replaces an abstract instruction. Memorable, immediately actionable.

**4. Self-repair that lands**
> "So the thing is — wait, let me back up. The real question isn't whether we can do it. It's whether the team has capacity to sustain it after launch."

- Setting: Strategy discussion
- Dimensions: Strategic Fluency (4), Discourse Coherence (4)
- Why it works: Classic achievement strategy — starts one direction, catches it, restructures, and lands a sharper point than the original attempt would have.

### Growth targets

**Do more of:**
- **Deploy metaphors in group settings, not just 1:1s.** The coaching conversation showed strong circumlocution (the movie trailer example). Transfer that skill into the higher-pressure group context where it's currently absent.
- **Use the "wait, let me back up" repair earlier.** The self-repair in moment #4 worked perfectly — but it came after 8 seconds of dissolution. The goal: catch the misfire in 3 seconds, not 8.

**Reduce:**
- **Trailing dissolves in cross-functional moments.** Two instances where a sentence started strong then trailed into "...and so on, you know what I mean" without landing the specific point. Replacement: pause, then restart with "The specific thing I mean is..."
